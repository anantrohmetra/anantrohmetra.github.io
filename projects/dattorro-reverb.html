Dattorro Plate Reverb (Pure Data Patch)

A Pure Data implementation of Jon Dattorro's plate reverb algorithm, built as part of an exploration into how digital reverberation creates a perceived sense of acoustic space.

Do spaces exist inside or outside our head? How do we get a sense of space using digital reverb?

About the Algorithm

The Dattorro reverb is a classic plate-style artificial reverb described in Jon Dattorro's 1997 paper. It models the acoustic behaviour of a vibrating metal plate using:

  • Input diffusion — four all-pass filters that smear the transient attack of the input signal
  • Decay tank — two coupled feedback delay lines with modulated all-pass filters, creating a dense, evolving tail
  • Modulation — two LFOs slightly detune the all-pass delays, adding shimmer and preventing metallic resonances

See history.md for the full lineage from Schroeder (1962) through the hardware era to Dattorro (1997).
